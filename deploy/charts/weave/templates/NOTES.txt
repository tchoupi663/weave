Weave is deployed as four services in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}:

  weave-live       Hubble + Beyla ingest, observed graph, snapshot/SSE
  weave-history    DuckDB metrics, incidents, evidence, timeline
  weave-inventory  Argo declared workloads, lifecycle, change events
  weave-api        Operator UI and the single browser origin

Open the UI:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/weave-api 8090:8080

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8090
